Welcome to Farewell Party Management System

The Farewell Party Management System is designed to streamline the organization of farewell events for senior students. With this system, junior students can easily plan memorable farewell events, covering aspects such as attendee management, task assignments, budget tracking, and detailed planning of dinner, stage performances, decorations, and venue arrangements.

Login

Menu Suggestions

Students can suggest items for the dinner menu, and a voting system ensures the most popular items are included in the final menu.

Performance Volunteer

Students can propose performances for the farewell party, and voting helps in selecting the most anticipated performances.

Budget Tracking

The system facilitates budget tracking for the farwell to monitor expenses related to venue booking, catering, decorations, etc.

Task Assignment

Organizers can assign tasks related to decorations, venue setup, and other responsibilities to student volunteers, with progress tracking and completion status visible.

Attendance Tracking

System administrators can track the attendance of students, teachers, and their families and generate reports on the number of attendees and any special requests or considerations.

Announcements

Stay informed about the latest updates and progress related to the farewell party by reading announcements from the organizing committee and checking task assignments.

Invitations

Design and distribute visually appealing invitations for the farewell party using the system's invitation management feature.
